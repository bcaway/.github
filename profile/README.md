# BCAway

The unofficial and easy way for Bergen County Academies students to see which teachers are absent.

## About

BCAway provides a simple way for students to see which teachers are away throughout the school day. Instead of having to figure out teacher absences through different sources, BCAway puts the information in one place and makes it easy to check by period.

## For the BCA Community

BCAway is an unofficial student-made project for the Bergen County Academies community :)

The goal is simple: make teacher absence information easier for students to find and understand throughout the school day.
